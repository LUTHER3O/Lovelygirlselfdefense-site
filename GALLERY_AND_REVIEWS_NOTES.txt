PRODUCT GALLERY UPDATE
=====================
All 86 uploaded product images are now integrated into product-page sliders.
Each product's galleryImages field in assets/products.json contains its
category-matched additional images. The original catalogue image remains first.
The product detail page shows thumbnails plus previous/next arrows and image zoom.

Category image grouping:
- Mystery Scoop: assortment and keychain contents; the press-on-nails scoop also
  includes the supplied nail reference images.
- Safety Keychain Set: matching multi-piece safety/keychain kit images.
- Single Safety Items: individual accessories, personal-safety items and drinkware.
- Fashion Bags: bag and tote images.
- False Nails: nail/product reference images where the category is used.

REAL CUSTOMER REVIEW ATTRIBUTION
================================
The product pages for Selfdefense Keychains Mystery Scoop and TOTE BAG include
short excerpts of actual public customer reviews found on the matching original
Extenify product pages. Each review block discloses that it was originally
published on Extenify and links to its source. These are NOT represented as
verified Lovely Girl Self Defense orders or newly submitted reviews.
No reviews have been invented for products without a source.

The "Leave a review" form remains a front-end placeholder. To collect and publish
new verified reviews, connect a review backend (for example WooCommerce reviews
or another review service) and moderate submissions.
