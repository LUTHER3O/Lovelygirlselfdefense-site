PRODUCT IMAGE SWIPE SELECTOR
===========================
Every product page now includes 4-6 additional images curated from the uploaded
86-image ZIP, matched to the closest relevant product style/category. The
existing main product image is slide 1, followed by those additional choices.

Buyer interaction:
- Swipe left/right directly on the large product photo on touch devices.
- Use the previous/next arrows on desktop or mobile.
- Tap a thumbnail to jump directly to an image.
- The counter and "Selected look" label update as the buyer moves between images.
- The active image choice is included in the cart/order options as
  "Selected photo / style", so the selected visual type is carried with the item.

Each product has 5-7 total slides (main image + 4-6 uploaded related images).
The images are stored in assets/images/product-options/.
See assets/products.json -> galleryImages for each product's image assignment.
